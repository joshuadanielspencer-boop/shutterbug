Avatar lab

The new plates stack with no anchoring or scaling at all — every delivery is the same 1200×1200 canvas and, within a family, the same alpha mask. Z-order is outfit → head → brow → eyes → hair. The eyebrow layer is not delivered art: it is lifted out of the head plates and recoloured to match the hair, so five small files cover every combination. Built by node scripts/build-avatar-layers.mjs.
